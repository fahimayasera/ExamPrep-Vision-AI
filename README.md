# ExamPrep Vision AI 📚

**From Your Final Notes to Smart Revision**

**Understand It. Revise It. Remember It.**

ExamPrep Vision AI is an AI-powered exam preparation and revision assistant that helps students use their own final study notes more effectively. It analyzes uploaded notes, helps students understand and remember important concepts, and generates mark-based exam questions for focused exam preparation.

## 🎯 Problem

Students often spend a lot of time preparing their final study notes. After completing them, they may have very little time left for effective revision.

ExamPrep Vision AI helps students make better use of their already-prepared final notes.

## 💡 Solution

The student uploads their own final study notes, and the AI helps them:

- Understand important concepts
- Identify important points for revision
- Check notes for missing concepts, repeated points, and unclear wording
- Generate exam questions based on marks
- Ask questions about their study notes through the chat box

## ✨ Features

### 📸 Upload Final Study Notes

Upload an image of your final study notes. The AI can read and understand the uploaded material.

### 📝 Smart Note Check

Review final notes and identify:

- Important concepts that may be missing
- Repeated points
- Unclear wording
- Key points that deserve more attention

The feature does not rewrite or remove the student's final notes.

### 📚 Generate Exam Questions

Generate exam-focused questions based on the required marks:

- **2 Marks** — Very short-answer question
- **3 Marks** — Short-answer question
- **4 Marks** — Short-answer question with slightly more detail
- **5 Marks** — Medium-answer question
- **10 Marks** — Detailed-answer question

### 💬 Study Chat

Ask questions about your study material or attach study-note images and get AI-powered academic help.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Enter your name and contact information.
2. Upload your final study notes.
3. Use Smart Note Check to review your notes.
4. Generate exam questions based on marks.
5. Use the chat box for study-related questions.
